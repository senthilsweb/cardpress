# A clearer studio

**Status:** the layout was accepted by the owner on 2026-11-25. Nothing is built.

## Why
The owner finds the studio confusing. What causes it:

1. **One item is edited in several places.** Card words are under Edit, Text; the post is under Edit, Write; the summary and address only appear when publishing.
2. **Two rows of switches** on the left (Chat | Edit, then four tabs) and two more above the canvas.
3. **Status is hidden.** Approve sits at the bottom of Write; what is missing is found by trying.
4. **Chat and item are loosely tied.** Two New buttons, Post / Article ticks that look like the canvas switch, and replies that are blocks of text.

## What changes
The header, left column and canvas stay where they are.

- Left column, one row: **Chat | Details | Design**.
- **Details** is one form for the item on screen, built from the content schema: every field once, grouped, with counters, and who approved it. Status and the next action stay pinned at the bottom.
- **Design** holds today's Design tab. Brand settings move to the brand menu and open in the right drawer.
- Above the canvas: the item, its status, its version, and one **New**.
- Chat replies become short cards: what was saved, the check result, and any options to choose.

## Out of scope
The Library, public pages, publishing rules and the look of cards.
