# Publishing calendar (future bolt)

**Status:** proposed for a later bolt. Specification only: nothing is built.

## Why
Posts can be scheduled and articles given a planned date, but only one item at a time from its preview. There is no view of what goes out when.

## What changes
- A **Calendar** page: month and week, showing scheduled, sent and failed posts and articles with a planned date, per brand or all the person's brands.
- **Schedule from the calendar:** pick a day, choose an approved post or article, set the time.
- **Links to it:** a Calendar entry in the account menu, and a calendar action next to each article in the Library list that opens the calendar with that article selected.

## Rules that stay
- Only an approved item can be scheduled; a person confirms every scheduled post.
- An article's date is a plan: nothing is sent to a network.
- A waiting post is locked; moving it is cancelling and scheduling again, in one confirmed step.

## Out of scope
Recurring posts, suggested times, drag-and-drop across brands, other people's items for non-admins, scheduling by the assistant.
