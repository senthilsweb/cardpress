# Greeting layouts

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Words are placed as a greeting
#### Scenario: Centred greeting
- **WHEN** the card uses Greeting · Centred
- **THEN** the lead words, headline, message and sign-off are stacked and centred, clear of the art

### Requirement: Text always fits
#### Scenario: A long headline
- **WHEN** the headline is too long for one line
- **THEN** it shrinks or wraps and nothing is cut off, at every card size

### Requirement: A template keeps the words
#### Scenario: Apply a template
- **WHEN** the person applies a greeting template
- **THEN** background, layout, colours and script font change, and the text does not
