# Backgrounds and profiles

> Backdated record, written 2026-11-22.

## Why
Cards need a recognisable look per brand without photos for every post.

## What changes
Eleven built-in backgrounds (constellation, dot grid, code lines, rising bars, geometric, contours, gradient mesh, photo mosaic, browser frame, own image, plain), all drawn from the palette with a seeded generator. Profiles carry logo, mark, fonts and palettes. The palettes are Paper, Ink, Deep teal, Teal paper and the brochure palettes (dark and light), with Aubergine `#4A154B` among them.
