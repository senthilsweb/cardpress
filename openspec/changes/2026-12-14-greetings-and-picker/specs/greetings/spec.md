# Greetings

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Greetings are kept and reused
#### Scenario: Changed list
- **WHEN** a greeting is added, edited or deleted
- **THEN** the next reading of the list shows the change

#### Scenario: Words stay unless asked
- **WHEN** a background is applied without selecting a greeting
- **THEN** the card's text is unchanged

#### Scenario: In chat
- **WHEN** the person asks for Diwali greetings
- **THEN** they are offered as headline options to pick from
