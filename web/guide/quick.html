CardPress quick guide

From an empty card to a published post. Open the full guide · Back to the studio

1. Choose the brand

Press the CardPress mark at the top left and pick a brand. Posts, designs and social accounts belong to the open brand.

2. Start a post

Press New above the card.

3. Write it

  • With chat: open Chat, tick Post, say what the post is about and press send. Pick any options offered, then press Save as version.
  • By hand: open Details and fill in the body and the card words.

4. Design the card

Open Design. Choose a layout, a background (Browse backgrounds) and colours.

5. Check it

Press the eye at the bottom of Details to see the post as it will look.

6. Approve and publish

  1. Press Approve.
  2. Press Publish.
  3. Choose Publish now… and confirm, or Schedule… and set the date, time and time zone.

CardPress publishes to LinkedIn. For other networks, press Download PNG and post by hand. LinkedIn is connected once per brand, under Settings → Social accounts.

7. Follow up

Calendar shows what is sent and what is waiting. Library lists every post and article.
