An article carries the detail; a post draws readers to it. Both can be made in one chat.

Make both with chat

  1. Press New, then open Chat.
  2. Tick Post and Article.
  3. Say the subject and attach material.
  4. Use Post, Article or Both above the card area to look at them. Both needs a wide screen.
  5. Ask for changes to either one: "shorten the post", "add a section about costs to the article".

One chat keeps one post and one article. Choosing either in the list above the card opens the chat about it.

Suggested order

StepWhat to do
1Finish and Approve the article
2Publish it: on LinkedIn by hand, or with Publish page…
3Copy the article's web address into the post
4Approve the post, then publish or schedule it

Any change to an approved post returns it to draft, so add the link before approving.

Plan them

Give the article a planned date and schedule the post. The Calendar shows both.
