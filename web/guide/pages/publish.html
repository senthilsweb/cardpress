Posts go to LinkedIn, now or at a chosen time. A person confirms every post. Articles are not sent automatically.

Before the first post

Connect LinkedIn for the brand.

Approve

Press Approve at the bottom of Details. Any later change returns the post to draft, so what was approved is what goes out.

Publish now

  1. Press Publish, or the eye to see the preview first.
  2. Press Publish now….
  3. Check the brand named, then press Yes, publish now.

Schedule

  1. In the preview press Schedule….
  2. Set the date, time and time zone: at least a minute ahead, within a year.
  3. Confirm.

A scheduled post is fixed at the moment of scheduling. To change it, cancel it and schedule again.

Publishing queue

Under Details → Publishing queue:

StatusMeaningNext step
ScheduledWaiting for its timePublish now, reschedule or cancel
PublishingBeing sentWait
PublishedSentView on LinkedIn
FailedNot posted. The reason is shown.Fix the cause, then publish again
MissedThe time passed while CardPress was not runningPublish now, reschedule or discard
Check LinkedInIt is not known whether the post went outLook at LinkedIn first. If the post is there, discard. If not, publish again.
CancelledCancelledSchedule again or discard

A failed post is never sent again automatically.

Change a sent post

A sent post is locked. Edit again or Back to draft starts a new version. The post already on LinkedIn does not change.

Articles

LinkedIn cannot receive an article from outside: see LinkedIn articles.
