CardPress makes social cards and posts in a brand's own colours and fonts, and publishes them to LinkedIn.

The screen

PartWhat it does
Left barBrand menu (top), Studio, Library, Calendar, Settings, Help, and the account menu (bottom)
ChatWrite and change a post by asking the assistant
DetailsEvery field of the post: text, card words, hashtags, planning, publishing
DesignHow the card looks
Card areaThe card or the post preview, sizes, and download

On a phone the left bar moves to the bottom.

First post in five steps

  1. Set up a brand.
  2. Connect LinkedIn for that brand.
  3. Create a card, or ask the assistant.
  4. Press Approve.
  5. Publish or schedule.
