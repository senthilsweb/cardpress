The assistant writes posts and articles into the draft and changes them on request. It cannot publish.

Ask for a draft

  1. Open Chat.
  2. Tick Post, Article or both.
  3. Say what it is about, then press send or Enter.

The first draft is checked for quality and can take a few minutes. Stop ends a reply.

Give it material

Press the paperclip to attach a PDF, Word, Markdown or text file (up to 10 MB), add a link to a public web page, or paste text.

Pick from options

Ask for choices, for example "three headlines". Select one to see it on the card. Nothing is saved yet.

Save, undo, keep

ButtonResult
Save as versionPuts the selected options into the post and saves a version
UndoReturns the post to how it was before that reply. Not possible after further editing.
Keep as isDrops the preview

Examples

  • "Write a post about our new report."
  • "Give me three headlines."
  • "Make it shorter."
  • "Add an eyebrow." and "Remove the eyebrow."
  • "Clear the left footer."

The assistant changes the words. Layout, background and colours are chosen under Design.

Chats

New starts a fresh chat and post. The chat's name at the top opens the list of chats, with search. Deleting a chat keeps the post it wrote. Notes shows what the assistant remembers about the brand; any note can be removed.

If the panel says no assistant is set up, ask the person who runs this CardPress.
