CardPress makes social cards, posts and articles in a brand's own colours and fonts, and publishes posts to LinkedIn.

The screen

PartWhat it does
Left barBrand menu (top), Studio, Library, Calendar, Settings, User guide, and the account menu (bottom)
ChatWrite and change a post or article by asking the assistant
DetailsEvery field: text, card words, hashtags, planning, publishing
DesignHow the card looks
Card areaThe card or the preview, sizes, and download

On a phone the left bar moves to the bottom.

Before the first post

  1. Set up a brand: name, logo, fonts and colours.
  2. Connect LinkedIn for that brand.

First post in six steps

  1. Check the open brand at the top left.
  2. Press New above the card.
  3. Write: open Chat, tick Post, say what the post is about and press send. Or type under Details.
  4. Design: open Design and choose a layout, a background and colours.
  5. Press Approve.
  6. Press Publish, then Publish now… or Schedule….
