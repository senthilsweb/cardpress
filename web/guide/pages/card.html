A card is the image that goes out with a post, and the cover of an article.

Start

Press New above the card. It opens an empty draft in the brand's look.

Card words

Under Details → Card words, type the text. The card changes while typing.

FieldWhere it shows
EyebrowSmall line above the headline
HeadlineThe large text. Enter starts a new line.
SubtitleBelow the headline. A | splits it into points.
Footer, left and FooterSmall text along the bottom
Tag, Number, Quote author, Sign-offUsed by some layouts

A footer that was never filled in shows the brand's website (left) or handle (right). A cleared footer stays empty. Leave empty and Use the brand's, under an empty field, switch between the two.

Design

  • Layout: Hero, Centred, Article cover, Quote, Big number, Headshot or Greeting.
  • Background: see Backgrounds and greetings. Shuffle rearranges it.
  • Images: add photos, a screenshot or a background image.
  • Colours: pick a colour set.
  • Fonts: change the fonts of this card only.
  • Fine tune: title size, background strength, bottom fade, show or hide the logo and footer.

Sizes and download

Choose a network and size above the card, or All sizes to see them together. Then use Download PNG, Copy or All sizes .zip.

Feed preview shows the post as it would look in the network's feed.

Versions

The version button above the card, such as v4, lists earlier versions. Make current brings one back.
