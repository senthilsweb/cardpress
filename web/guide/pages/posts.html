A post is short text that goes to LinkedIn with a card, images, a PDF or a video link.

Write a post with chat

  1. Press New, then open Chat.
  2. Tick Post.
  3. Say what it is about. Attach a file, a link or pasted text with the paperclip if the post should draw on it.
  4. Pick among any options offered, then press Save as version.
  5. Ask for changes: "shorter", "give me three headlines", "start with the result".

Write a post by hand

  1. Under Details → Writing, choose Post and type the body.
  2. Under Card words, fill in the text for the card.
  3. Under Sharing, add hashtags and alt text (a short description of the image for people who cannot see it).

Length and layout

  • LinkedIn allows 3,000 characters, hashtags included.
  • The feed shows about the first three lines before "…see more", so put the point first.
  • A counter shows the length and a suggested aim. The aim never blocks publishing.
  • LinkedIn shows plain text. Lists are kept; bold and italic are left out unless the option for them is switched on.

Images, PDF or video instead of the card

Under Sharing → Attachment, choose Use images or a PDF, or add a video link. Use the card goes back to the card.

Check and publish

Press the eye to see the post as LinkedIn will show it. Then Approve and publish or schedule.
