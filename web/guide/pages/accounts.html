Each person signs in with their own account.

Sign in

  1. Enter the email address and password.
  2. Press Sign in.

Where sign-up is open, Create an account is on the same page. Otherwise an admin adds the account.

A password has at least 10 characters. A few words together work well.

After five wrong passwords, sign-in pauses for 15 minutes. No password reminder is sent by email: an admin sets a new password.

Account settings

Settings → Account changes the name, phone, date of birth and password. Changing the password signs out other devices.

The account menu at the bottom left switches between light and dark, and signs out.

Manage people (admins)

Settings → Users adds a person, changes a role, disables or enables an account, and sets a new password.
