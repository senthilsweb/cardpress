Make a greeting card

  1. Press New.
  2. Design → Layout → Greeting. The card becomes square, unless another size was chosen.
  3. Design → Background → Browse backgrounds, search for the occasion, select one and press Use. Use matching colours sets the colours too.
  4. Fill in the words under Details → Card words, or ask in the chat.
  5. Write the post, then Approve and publish or Schedule… for the day.

The greeting layout

FieldWhere it showsExample
EyebrowSmall, widely spaced words at the topHappy
HeadlineLarge script. It shrinks to fit.Thanksgiving!
SubtitleThe message, centredWith gratitude for everyone we serve.
Sign-offScript, lower right. Enter starts a new line.From all of us at TemplrPress

Design → Ornament picks the drawing under the headline: Heart flourish, Plain divider, Leaf sprig, Diya, Sparkles, Plum blossom or None. It takes the card's colours, and the ones suiting the background's occasion are listed first. The script font is set under Design → Fonts. Harvest frame leaves the middle of the card clear for the words.

Design → Background → Shading sets how strong the wash behind the words is, from 0% (the art untouched) to 100%. Harvest frame and Plain need none. Words that are too tall for the card are made smaller to fit.

With chat

Open Chat, tick Post and ask, for example:

  • "Write a short Thanksgiving post to our readers."
  • "Give me three Thanksgiving headlines."
  • "Add a subtitle thanking our customers."
  • "Remove the eyebrow and clear the left footer."

Select a headline to see it on the card, then Save as version. The chat writes the words; the background and colours are chosen under Design. Choosing a background never changes the words.

New backgrounds can be plugged in. Each background is a small add-on. Adding one takes little effort, and it then becomes available to everyone using this CardPress: ask the person who runs it. For a one-off design, any custom picture can be used instead: add it under Design → Images → Background image and choose Own image.
