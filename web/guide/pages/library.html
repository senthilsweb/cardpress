The Library lists everything drafted, scheduled and posted, newest first.

Library → Media keeps a brand's pictures, PDF documents and video links. Press Upload or drop files on the page; add a video with Add video link. Large pictures are made smaller, and a photo's own data, such as where it was taken, is left out. Long libraries come in pages.

Wherever you add a picture to a card or a brand, choose Upload (it is added to the library) or From library. A post's own images or PDF can come From library too. A file that a card or a saved version uses cannot be deleted; the message says where it is used.

Find an item

  • Search titles, text and hashtags.
  • Filter by brand, type and status.
  • Switch between Grid and List. In the grid, press a column heading to sort.

Open and act

Press a row to see the card and the full text. The row menu (⋯) offers View, Edit, Schedule… or Plan a date…, Back to draft and Delete.

Delete

Only a draft can be deleted: bring an approved item back to draft first, and cancel a scheduled post first. Deleting the record of a sent post leaves the post on LinkedIn.
