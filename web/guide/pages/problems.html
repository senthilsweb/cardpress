No Publish now button

  • The post is not approved: press Approve.
  • LinkedIn is not connected for this brand: see Connect social accounts.
  • The account is a writer: an editor or admin publishes.

LinkedIn refuses a post

Open Settings → Social accounts → LinkedIn and press Test connection. An expired token is the usual cause: make a new one.

A post shows Check LinkedIn

Look at the LinkedIn page before doing anything. Publishing again can post twice.

The wrong brand, logo or colours

Check the open brand at the top left. A post stays with the brand it was made in.

A post cannot be edited

It is waiting to be sent, or already sent. Cancel it in the queue, or press Back to draft.

The footer shows unwanted text

Clear the field under Details → Card words, or press Leave empty. To hide both footers, switch off Show footer under Design → Fine tune.

The assistant does not reply

Press Stop, then start a New chat. A chat can also reach its spending limit; a new one starts again.

Sign-in does not work

Wait 15 minutes after several wrong passwords, or ask an admin to set a new password.
