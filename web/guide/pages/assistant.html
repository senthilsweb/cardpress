The chat's assistant can be set up for each brand: how strong it is, how drafts are checked, what it may spend and how it writes.

Tier, checks and budget

An administrator opens Settings → Brand assistant for the open brand.

SettingWhat it does
TierStronger models write better and cost more. New chats use the tier; open chats keep theirs.
Check first draftsA grader reads the first draft and may send it back, up to the rounds chosen. Off is quicker and cheaper.
Monthly budgetWhat the assistant may cost this brand in a calendar month. Empty for no limit.
Alert atThe share of the budget at which the chat warns.

When a budget runs out

The assistant stops, and new messages are refused until the budget is raised or the next month begins. The bar under Brand assistant and on the Dashboard shows what is spent. A reply in progress can end a little over the budget.

An administrator can also limit all brands together under Settings → Assistant.

Writing guides

Under Settings → Writing guides the brand's owner or an administrator says how the brand writes: Posts, Articles and Greetings each have their own guide. A guide replaces the built-in style for that kind.

  1. Press Start from ours, or write your own in Markdown. The preview shows how it reads.
  2. Say who reads it, the voice, the structure, words to use or avoid, and hashtags or a sign-off.
  3. Press Save.

A guide may only say how to write. Length limits, how drafts are saved and the safety rules always apply. A guide with HTML, code blocks, tool names or other instructions is refused, with the reason.
