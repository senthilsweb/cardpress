An article is long writing with headings, pictures and tables. The card is its cover. CardPress prepares the article; it is pasted into LinkedIn's article editor by hand.

Write an article with chat

  1. Press New, then open Chat.
  2. Tick Article and leave Post unticked.
  3. Say the subject and attach material: reports, notes, links.
  4. The first draft takes a few minutes and is checked for quality.
  5. Pick among title options, press Save as version, and ask for changes.

Write and edit by hand

Under Details → Writing, choose Article, then expand the editor with the arrows.

  • The toolbar has headings, bold, italic, links, quotes, code, pictures, tables and dividers.
  • Type / on an empty line to insert a block.
  • Paste or drop pictures straight into the text.
  • The outline on the left jumps between headings. Word count and reading time are at the bottom.

Cover card

The card words and the design make the cover. The Article cover layout suits it. Title names the article; Headline is the text on the card.

Put it on LinkedIn

  1. Approve the article.
  2. Open the preview and press For LinkedIn.
  3. In LinkedIn, start a new article.
  4. Copy the title and the body with their Copy buttons and paste them.
  5. Download the pictures and add each where its marker is. Upload the card as the cover.

Tables become lists and dividers are dropped, because LinkedIn's editor has no place for them. The window says what changed.

Other ways to share

  • Planned date: shows the article on the calendar. Nothing is sent.
  • Markdown or HTML: downloads the article for a blog.
  • Publish page…: puts an approved article on a public web page of this CardPress, with its card as the link preview. Take down removes it.
